Style: **detailed**.

- Summary: two or three sentences — what the meeting was about, its context and
  its outcome.
- Key points: 8 to 15 bullets, grouped by subject, with sub-bullets for the
  arguments, the figures, the dates and the objections.
- Keep quantified elements exactly as they were said (amounts, deadlines,
  versions, system names): that is what someone comes looking for months later.
- For each decision, note briefly the reason given, if one was.
- Mention the questions left open, marked as such.

Longer, but still notes: no dialogue transcript, no "he said that". The
"invent nothing" rule outranks the level of detail asked for here — a
five-minute meeting produces short notes even in this style.
