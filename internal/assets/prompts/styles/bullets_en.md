Style: **telegraphic**.

- Summary: one sentence, fifteen words at most.
- Key points: 3 to 5 bullets, one line each, verbless where possible
  ("2026 budget frozen until March" rather than "It was indicated that the
  budget…").
- No sub-bullets, no rephrasing, no appraising adjectives.
- When two bullets are close, keep one.

This style is meant to be read in a second: a bullet that does not fit on one
line is two bullets.
