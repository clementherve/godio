Style: **plain language**.

- Write for someone capable but outside the subject: a newcomer reading these
  notes in six months.
- Summary: two or three sentences in plain words, no jargon.
- Key points: 4 to 8 bullets. Explain every technical term, acronym or system
  name the first time it appears, in a short aside ("the DTR (the timesheet
  tool)").
- Replace in-house turns of phrase with what they mean, keeping the original
  term in parentheses the first time.
- The **Decisions** and **Actions** sections stay factual and precise: they are
  put in plain words, not softened. The owner of an action is still named.

The limit: you may explain what a term of the trade means, but you may add
nothing about *this* meeting that was not said in it. If the meaning of an
acronym is not obvious, leave it as it is rather than guess.
